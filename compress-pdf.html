How to compress a PDF without uploading it

  1. Download and open Free PDF Toolkit. It runs entirely on your PC.
  2. Go to the Compress tab and click Add files.
  3. Pick a compression level: low (structure only), medium or high (downsamples images by 4×).
  4. Press Compress. Your optimized PDF is saved next to the original with a _compressed suffix.

Why not use an online PDF compressor?

Online tools require you to upload your file to someone else's server. That is a problem for contracts, invoices, medical records and anything confidential. Free PDF Toolkit never sends your files anywhere — there is no network code in the app at all.

You also get no page limits, no watermarks and no "upgrade to remove restrictions" popups. See all five tools →

Will compression reduce quality?

Low and medium keep text and vector graphics untouched. High resizes embedded images, so use it for email and web — not for print.

How small can a PDF get?

It depends on the images inside. Scanned documents usually shrink the most; text-only PDFs shrink less.

Is there a file size limit?

No. You can process files of any size, limited only by your computer's memory.
