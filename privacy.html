Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

The application

Free PDF Toolkit is a desktop application that runs entirely on your computer. It contains no network or telemetry code. The PDF files you open are read from and written to your own disk only. We do not receive, upload, store or inspect your documents.

The app has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising embedded in it.

This website

This site is hosted on a static hosting provider. Like most websites, the host may keep standard server access logs (IP address, browser type, time of request) for security and maintenance. These logs are not used to identify you.

If advertising is enabled on this site in the future, third-party providers such as Google may use cookies to serve ads. You can disable cookies in your browser at any time. We do not sell your personal data.

If you have questions about this policy, please use the contact page.
